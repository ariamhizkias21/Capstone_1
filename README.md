# **Capstone 1**
## **East Region In-Store Sales Analysis**

## **Project Overview**

This project analyzes in-store sales for EmporiUm, a student bookstore chain. The analysis focuses on the East Region, which includes both New York and Connecticut. 
The purpose of this project is to understand sales performance, identify trends, compare store results, and provide business recommendations based on the data. 


## **Business Questions**
The analysis answers the following questions:

1. What is the total revenue for the East Region, and what is the date range of the sales data?

2. How does monthly revenue change over time?
   
3. Which state generates the most revenue in the East Region?
 
4. How do monthly sales transaction counts and average sales amounts compare across product categories?
 
5. Which stores have the highest and lowest total revenue?
 
6. How do product categories compare in the top-performing and lowest-performing stores, and where should management focus sales attention next quarter?

   
## **Key Findings**

Total East Region revenue was **$6,723,039.53**.
- The sales data covers **January 1, 2022**, through **December 31, 2025**.
- New York generated **$4,330,817.09**, while Connecticut generated **$2,392,222.44**.
- Annual revenue increased from **$1,086,634.60** in **2022** to **$2,436,605.47** in **2025**.
- October **2025** had the highest monthly revenue, at **$301,070.13**.
- The New York store was the highest-performing store, with **$621,565.02** in revenue.
- The Old Saybrook store in Connecticut was the lowest-performing store, with **$277,310.18** in revenue.
- Technology & Accessories generated the highest revenue in both stores, followed by Textbooks.
  
## **Business Recommendations**

Based on my analysis, I recommend that management focus on improving sales at lower-performing stores, especially Old Saybrook, Darien and Litchfield County. They should look at which products customers are buying, whether popular products are available, and what could help increase sales. Technology and Accessories had the highest revenue in both the New York and Old Saybrook stores, so management should continue supporting this category.They can also look at successful stores like New York and Rochester to see what they are doing well and apply similar strategies to other locations.
